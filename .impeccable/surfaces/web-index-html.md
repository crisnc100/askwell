# Askwell front page

## Scope and mode
The new front page at `/` (Persuade). The working ask flow moves to `/try/`; Results (`/results/`) and Value (`/value/`) become the details behind it and inherit this world later.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints
- Hiring managers and business leaders or owners weighing Cristian as an applied-AI builder or consultant; second, small teams with data to try.
- They must understand in seconds how the AI is used here, what it is not used for, and what the method achieves.
- Primary action: ask the demo database a question (working, on the page), then "Try it on your data" (`/try/`).
- Proof: a real recorded question answered with its form, the carbon copy of what the AI saw, the SQL and the rows; the three measured numbers from ADR-008 (62 of 96 never-seen questions right; wrong about 1 in 25, was 1 in 14; it refused all 72 it should). No invented customers or claims.
- Avoid: salesy hype, the AI-startup look (gradients, glow, chat bubbles, sparkles), busy pages. Less is more.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Every answer is a cheque with its carbon copy: what you asked, what the AI was shown, and the stamped, checked result. It refuses the AI landing page (headline beside a chat screenshot, a row of feature cards).
OWN-WORLD: Sage cheque paper with a fine security pattern as the ground; ink near-black; carbon indigo for everything the AI saw; ledger red for rules and the CHECKED stamp; ledger blue for form rules. Grotesk type, rank by weight and case at a few sizes. Hard 2px ink rules, a dashed perforation between original and carbon, no shadows or rounded cards.
STORY: The visitor reads the promise, watches one question go through the slip, sees the AI was shown names only (empty carbon cells, 0 rows), sees the SQL and the stamped answer, reads the three numbers, then tries a question or their own data.
