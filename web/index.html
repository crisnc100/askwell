      Why the AI only fills in a form

      Understanding a question is the hard part, and that is the AI's job. Writing and running the query has to be exact, so plain code does it. That split is why every answer can be checked, and why it says so when it can't answer safely.

      What the AI doesWhat it never does
      Understands a question in your own words.Never sees a row of your data.
      Maps your words onto your tables, columns and values: “still unpaid” becomes status = unpaid.Never writes SQL. Plain code writes it from the form and runs it read-only.
      Works only from what it is shown: table and column names, each column's type and short description, and category labels.Never sees hidden columns.
      Tries once more, with the check's reasons, when the check turns its form down.Never guesses when unsure. It says it can't answer.
